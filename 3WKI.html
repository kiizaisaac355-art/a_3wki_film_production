1. PROTECT AND SERVE / NIGHT RUN - HALFWAY

Elijah, a police officer and genius programmer, gets a call from a young boy who designed software to improve security of the country. Bad people want it. TRUST IS THE ONLY ESCAPE.

2. HANDSOME SIBLINGS - FINISHED

Ryan and Kylian, twin brothers, grew up with their uncle after their parents were killed by Charles. BROTHERS IN BLUE. PROTECTORS FOREVER.

3. JOSHUA THE FREEDOM FIGHTER - FINISHED

Joshua, Joab and Sharom fought to reveal truth on a case of 15 years back that took General John's family. Mzee James saved them. BROTHERHOOD. HONOR. DESTINY.

4. ALIEN SUCCESSOR - HALFWAY

Brandon grew up in a remote forest. He found a monster, helped it, and it entered his body and they became one person.

5. SECRET MISSION - FINISHED

Alvin, a soldier on secret mission, meets Angel, a news reporter, at airport with an accidental kiss. Later they find they are neighbours and have same mission.

6. LOVE IS THE MOST BEAUTIFUL THING IN LIFE - FINISHED

Gracious and Baolar - grandparents decided they should marry. They met at game competition. God's timing is best. TOGETHER IS THE BEST PLACE TO BE.

7. ISHMAEL THE SKILFUL HUNTER - FINISHED

Ishmael, son of Hagar, grew up with wild animals, became skilful hunter. He saved his girlfriend from looters with battalion of wild animals. NATURE FEARS HIM.

8. DREAMS COME TRUE - FINISHED

Agaba and Amon dreamed to become superstars like Mr. John and Sheila. By God's grace he fulfilled dream. SOME MOMENTS ARE WORTH IT.
